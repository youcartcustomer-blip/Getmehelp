We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your email is never shared or sold — it is used only for GetMeHelp offer notifications.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our site and our advertising partners' links may use cookies or similar tracking technology to attribute clicks and purchases for commission purposes. When you click an offer on GetMeHelp, you may be redirected through a tracking link (e.g. via our affiliate network partner, Awin) before reaching the advertiser's website. This is standard practice for affiliate marketing and does not expose any personal data beyond what the advertiser's own site collects directly from you.

4. Affiliate Disclosure

GetMeHelp participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn a commission when you click a link or make a purchase through one of our offers, at no extra cost to you. This does not influence our recommendations, but transparency matters to us — every offer page and ad we run discloses this relationship.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party advertisers and services (e.g. app stores, merchant websites). Once you leave getmehelp.vercel.app, that third party's own privacy policy governs any data they collect. We encourage you to review their policies separately.

6. Your Choices

  • You can unsubscribe from offer emails at any time via the link in any email we send.
  • You can clear cookies in your browser at any time to limit tracking.
